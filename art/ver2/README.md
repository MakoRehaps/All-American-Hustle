# All American Hustle — Art Ver2

This branch is the working pass for the four player characters.

## Locked direction

- **ArnoldBacks** — preserve the existing Paintown-scale body and costume identity, keep the 90s visor covering the eyes, and push the face/build toward an Arnold Schwarzenegger-style action-star parody without changing him into a different character. Power/grappler moveset.
- **JohnDutch** — preserve the approved black-jacket/white-shirt street look and push the face/body toward a Jean-Claude Van Damme-style action-star parody. Fast kickboxer/rushdown moveset.
- **StevenStegals** — preserve the original suited player art and proportions. Technical counter / joint-lock fighter; do not redesign into a generic secret agent.
- **sylvestermalone** — preserve the original tactical/armored player art and proportions. Aggressive/tactical brawler; do not redesign into a generic soldier.

## Art rules

1. Existing player artwork is the source of truth.
2. New art should add poses, transitions, attacks, specials, grabs, throws, hurt/fall/rise/KO frames — not replace the character identity.
3. Keep consistent sprite scale, canvas dimensions, feet/origin alignment, shading style and transparent backgrounds for Paintown.
4. ArnoldBacks keeps the visor in every applicable frame.
5. The gameplay heart is River City Ransom-style beat-'em-up RPG, but the art remains the game's own larger, detailed Paintown-style characters.

## Story context

The game opens on the set of the in-universe action movie **Only Action 2 1/2**. The four action-star parodies begin by fighting stunt performers before the movie shoot turns into a real action adventure.
